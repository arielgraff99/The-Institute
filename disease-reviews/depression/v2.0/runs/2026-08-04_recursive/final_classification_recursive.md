Run date: 2026-08-04  
Historical ruler: frozen Historical Disease-Splitting Framework v0.2  
Non-psychiatric holdout: v0.2-H1  
Scope: recursive web-accessible evidence review with study-family deduplication and saturation stopping; not a licensed-database systematic review.

## Result

**Preferred representation: COMPOSITE_MULTIAXIAL.**

The recursive run expanded the evidence set from 35 to **58 records** and from 33 to **55 study families**. The higher-confidence result remains:

- DISTINCT_DISEASE — **NOT SUPPORTED AS A GLOBAL TRD CLASS**
- BIOLOGICAL_SUBTYPE_OR_ENDOTYPE — **NOT SUPPORTED AS THE GLOBAL TRD CLASS**
- DIMENSION_OR_SPECTRUM — **PARTIALLY SUPPORTED**
- DISEASE_STAGE — **PARTIALLY SUPPORTED**
- TREATMENT_RESPONSE_STATE — **PARTIALLY SUPPORTED**
- OPERATIONAL_CLINICAL_CATEGORY — **SUPPORTED**

The same class-level result was obtained with the non-psychiatric historical holdout.

## What changed relative to the sentinel v2.0 run

### 1. MDD-wide endotypes became more credible

The expanded search found large and/or independently validated neuroimaging subtypes, multi-omic immune/metabolic subtypes, EEG response markers, genetic treatment-complexity signals, early inflammatory biology and symptom-direction genetic structure. These findings strengthen **biological heterogeneity within MDD**.

They do **not** positively define the entire TRD population. For that reason the recursive adjudication corrects E01 for the *global TRD endotype hypothesis* to **NOT_MET**: candidate endotypes are cross-cutting alternatives to a single TRD endotype, not evidence for one.

### 2. Pretreatment temporality improved but remains insufficient for a disease boundary

There are **32 biological records** in the expanded ledger. **16** are baseline or pre-resistance predictors, compared with **8** established-TRD/post-treatment or consequence-uncertain records and **7** mixed/unknown records.

This is an improvement over the sentinel run, but the pretreatment signals predict *response heterogeneity*, not a reproducible categorical transition into one TRD disease.

### 3. Boundary robustness weakened further

Multiple independent definition studies show that who is called TRD changes materially with the number/type of treatment switches, augmentation rules, dose/duration requirements and available treatment history. The two-failure threshold remains clinically useful but behaves as an operational cut-point rather than a demonstrated biological discontinuity.

### 4. Pseudoresistance remains unresolved rather than assumed away

The expanded run contains **9 records** that directly or partially address adherence and **26** that address treatment adequacy. Medication nonadherence is common in MDD, and direct serum-level evidence shows response differs with measured adherence. Diagnostic bipolarity, treatment access, provider behavior and incomplete exposure data remain competing explanations in subsets. Therefore R02 remains **PARTIALLY_MET**.

### 5. Treatment biomarkers are moving toward decision testing, with mixed results

Recent prospective trials are now testing whether biomarkers actually improve treatment choice. Some stratification signals replicate; however, biomarker-concordant drug assignment has not consistently improved the primary outcome, and pharmacogenomic guidance shows time-dependent rather than immediate benefit. This supports precision-treatment research but does not establish a separate TRD disease.

## Five-priority modules

### Treatment trajectory

39 of 58 records can be placed on an explicit treatment-course state. The evidence supports graded accumulation of treatment failure/chronicity and does not reveal a natural discontinuity at two failed antidepressants.

### Temporality

Pretreatment and first-line predictor evidence is now materially larger. It strengthens prediction/endotype research, but no stable pretreatment discriminator has been shown to identify the future global TRD population across treatments and settings.

### Pseudoresistance

R02 = **PARTIALLY_MET**. The literature still does not consistently verify adequate exposure, adherence, diagnosis, access and system causes simultaneously.

### Endotypes independent of TRD

28 MDD-wide or cross-cutting candidate-endotype records are represented. The result is **PROVISIONAL MDD ENDOTYPE STRUCTURE**, while `global_trd_equals_single_endotype = NOT_SUPPORTED`.

### Non-psychiatric historical holdout

The full and holdout rulers produce the same classification. The conclusion therefore does not depend on psychiatric historical precedents used to derive the general criteria.

## Saturation

Seven recursive search cycles were executed with 54 exact search queries. The primary classification did not change after the initial expansion and remained unchanged through the final six cycles. The prespecified stopping condition of at least three consecutive cycles without a conclusion-changing study family was therefore met within the web-accessible scope.

## Current scientific formulation

The best-supported model is:

**MDD contains multiple biological and clinical endotypes/response profiles. These interact with illness severity, chronicity, comorbidity, social and healthcare context, treatment selection, exposure and adherence to generate a longitudinal treatment-course trajectory. TRD is a useful later-course operational/treatment-response state imposed on that heterogeneous trajectory, not one demonstrated biological disease.**

## Highest-information missing evidence

A decisive study would enroll treatment-naive first-episode MDD, obtain repeated multimodal biological and contextual measures, provide standardized sequential treatments with direct adherence/exposure verification, freeze candidate endotypes before external validation, and test whether a baseline subtype predicts both multi-step resistance and differential treatment benefit across independent settings.

## Limitations

This run is broader and recursively saturated for the **primary classification** within web-accessible evidence, but it is not a formal exhaustive systematic review across licensed databases. It did not include duplicate human screening or a study-family-reconstructed meta-analysis of every eligible effect estimate.
