# Depression Disease-Specific Review Module v2.0 — Five-Priority Run

Run date: 2026-08-04  
Historical ruler: frozen Historical Disease-Splitting Framework v0.2  
Holdout sensitivity ruler: v0.2-H1 (psychiatric/developmental historical comparators excluded)  
Scope: executable sentinel v2.0 run; not yet an exhaustive systematic review.

## Primary classification

**Preferred representation: COMPOSITE_MULTIAXIAL.**

TRD remains strongly supported as an **OPERATIONAL_CLINICAL_CATEGORY** and partially supported as a **TREATMENT_RESPONSE_STATE**, **DIMENSION_OR_SPECTRUM**, and **DISEASE_STAGE** component. It is not supported as one **DISTINCT_DISEASE** or as one global **BIOLOGICAL_SUBTYPE_OR_ENDOTYPE**.

The new v2.0 result is more specific than v1.0: **biological endotypes within MDD are provisionally supported, but those endotypes do not map one-to-one onto TRD**.

## 1. Treatment-course trajectory

The 35-record ledger contains evidence at multiple trajectory positions rather than one binary TRD state. Twenty-two records can be placed on an explicit ordered treatment-course position; 13 remain mixed/unknown. Longitudinal evidence from STAR*D, incident-TRD trajectories, GSRD predictors and the 2026 episode-wide Maudsley staging study supports a graded accumulation of resistance, severity and chronicity rather than a demonstrated natural discontinuity at two failed treatments.

v2.0 therefore treats the common two-failure boundary as an operational threshold unless a natural biological discontinuity is independently demonstrated.

## 2. Temporality

Among 17 biological records, only a minority provide baseline or pre-resistance evidence. Five are coded as baseline/pre-resistance predictors, seven as established-TRD correlates or post-treatment/consequence-uncertain, and five as mixed/unknown.

This asymmetry materially limits distinct-disease inference. A biological difference measured after years of illness, multiple medications, ECT or other advanced interventions cannot by itself establish that the difference existed before resistance.

## 3. Pseudoresistance and alternative causes

The v2.0 engine treats pseudoresistance as a competing causal model rather than a generic limitation. It explicitly tracks inadequate exposure, nonadherence, intolerance, pharmacokinetic variability, diagnostic error/bipolarity, medical/substance causes, treatment unavailability/delay, and clinician/health-system effects.

Only a small part of the literature directly addresses adherence, while more studies address treatment adequacy. R02 therefore remains **PARTIALLY_MET**, not MET. This is why TREATMENT_RESPONSE_STATE remains partially rather than fully supported under the frozen historical gate despite strong clinical usefulness.

## 4. Endotypes independent of TRD

When the search is not conditioned on TRD membership, MDD-wide biological subgroup signals become more visible:

- neuroimaging/multi-omics subtypes with different genetic/synaptic and inflammatory patterns;
- independently validated metabolomic subtypes;
- immune-inflammatory-metabolic subtypes associated with different 8-week antidepressant outcomes;
- systemic immune/type-2 inflammatory signals in subsets of MDD;
- earlier treatment-moderating inflammatory findings such as GENDEP.

These findings justify **PROVISIONALLY_SUPPORTED MDD ENDOTYPE STRUCTURE**. They do not justify a claim that TRD is one endotype. Several candidate endotypes appear to cross the TRD/non-TRD boundary, and most require external replication and prospective treatment-interaction validation.

## 5. Non-psychiatric historical holdout

The holdout sensitivity excludes the historical schizophrenia-subtype case (HIST008), the PDD/autism subtype merger (HIST019), and the Kraepelinian schizophrenia-versus-affective-psychosis boundary (HIST035).

Every promoted scientific criterion used by the depression module retained explicit support from non-psychiatric historical cases. Applying the holdout ruler produced the same class-level depression result:

- DISTINCT_DISEASE — NOT_SUPPORTED_AS_GLOBAL_CLASS
- BIOLOGICAL_SUBTYPE_OR_ENDOTYPE — NOT_SUPPORTED_AS_GLOBAL_TRD_CLASS
- DIMENSION_OR_SPECTRUM — PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED
- DISEASE_STAGE — PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED
- TREATMENT_RESPONSE_STATE — PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED
- OPERATIONAL_CLINICAL_CATEGORY — SUPPORTED

Thus the conclusion is not dependent on psychiatric historical comparators in this sensitivity analysis.

## Important distinction introduced by v2.0

Two propositions are now evaluated separately:

1. **Does depression contain reproducible biological endotypes?** — provisionally yes.
2. **Is TRD itself one reproducible biological endotype or distinct disease?** — not supported.

This separation prevents a real inflammatory, metabolic, circuit or molecular subgroup from being incorrectly used as evidence that every patient meeting the operational TRD label shares that biology.

## Current causal representation

A more coherent model is:

baseline depressive liability + one or more biological endotypes + severity/chronicity + comorbidity + social/health-system context + treatment selection/exposure/adherence

→ treatment-course trajectory

→ remission / partial response / sequential nonresponse

→ operational TRD label

with additional illness and treatment effects potentially altering later biomarkers.

## v2.0 determination

**Strongly supported:** TRD is a clinically consequential operational category.  
**Partially supported:** treatment-response state, graded resistance dimension, stage-like accumulation.  
**Provisionally supported:** multiple biological endotypes within MDD, some treatment-relevant.  
**Not supported:** a single biological TRD endotype or one distinct TRD disease.  
**Stable under non-psychiatric historical holdout:** yes.

## Limitation

This run uses 35 purposively selected sentinel records from 33 study families. v2.0 validates the new causal/data architecture, but a production scientific conclusion still requires the full recursive database/registry search, complete study-family reconstruction, systematic extraction of treatment adequacy/adherence, and external validation of MDD-wide endotypes.
