# Search provenance — recursive schizophrenia run

Search date: 2026-08-04.

The run used iterative live web searches emphasizing PubMed/PMC, major peer-reviewed publishers, systematic reviews and prospective/longitudinal evidence. Search branches deliberately included both positive subtype evidence and contradiction/failed-replication evidence.

## Main query families

1. B-SNIP psychosis Biotypes: replication, imaging, familial, genetics and prospective clinical utility.
2. First-episode/early-course predictors of treatment-resistant schizophrenia, including dopamine, CSF, genetics and treatment response.
3. Primary/deficit negative symptoms: biological correlates, clustering, longitudinal stability and treatment response.
4. Cognitive/executive-function subtypes: independent validation, imaging/immune-metabolic correlates and prognosis.
5. Immune/inflammatory TRS/endotypes: response-stratified biomarkers and systematic reviews.
6. Neuroimaging subtypes: large positive clustering/SuStaIn studies versus field-level reproducibility critiques.
7. Rare etiologies: 22q11.2 deletion syndrome, GRIN2A and other pathogenic variants, precision-treatment implications.
8. Autoimmune psychosis/encephalitis in first-episode and persistent psychosis.
9. Cross-disorder genetics testing whether schizophrenia-specific biological boundaries persist against bipolar/other psychosis overlap.
10. Final contradiction/replication sweeps for all branches.

Approximately 70 search executions were reconstructed from the session across recursive acquisition and final source verification. Because tool context was compacted during the long run, early-cycle queries are represented here as normalized query families rather than falsely claimed as byte-exact raw strings. Later cycle queries were directly retained in session history.

No exact database hit counts, token counts, cost values or wall-time values are reported because they were not available in a trustworthy machine-readable form.

## Stopping

The last material subtype refinement occurred in cycle 7. Cycles 8, 9 and 10 specifically searched for external replication, contradictory reviews, prospective decision utility and etiologic exceptions and did not change a class-level conclusion. This satisfied the prespecified three-cycle stopping rule for the primary classification within the web-accessible scope.
