## Purpose

Expand the schizophrenia subtype review recursively until the prespecified stopping rule is met, while deliberately searching for evidence that could overturn the sentinel conclusion.

Priority branches:

- transdiagnostic psychosis Biotypes and external validation;
- first-episode predictors of treatment-resistant schizophrenia (TRS);
- primary/deficit negative-symptom biology;
- cognitive and executive-function subtypes;
- immune/inflammatory endotypes;
- neuroimaging subtype replication and contradiction;
- rare etiologic/monogenic and autoimmune causes that can present with schizophrenia-spectrum psychosis.

## Primary result

Preferred representation: `COMPOSITE_MULTIAXIAL / HETEROGENEOUS_SYNDROME`.

The recursive expansion does not revive traditional symptom-based schizophrenia subtypes and does not identify one global biological subdivision of schizophrenia. It does refine the model by adding an etiologic layer: rare genetically or immunologically defined diseases can present within a schizophrenia-spectrum phenotype and should be separated etiologically when demonstrated.

Current hierarchy:

1. `RARE_ETIOLOGICAL_DISEASE_SUBGROUPS_PRESENT` — SUPPORTED as exceptions/etiologic diagnoses, not as a complete schizophrenia taxonomy.
2. `TRANSDIAGNOSTIC_PSYCHOSIS_ENDOTYPES` — STRONGLY PROVISIONAL; B-SNIP remains the strongest replicated biomarker-defined structure but crosses DSM diagnoses.
3. `SCHIZOPHRENIA_INTERNAL_COGNITIVE_SUBTYPES` — PROVISIONALLY SUPPORTED; 2026 EF-Focal/EF-Global work adds longitudinal stability, local independent validation, biological correlates and prognostic separation.
4. `TRS_TREATMENT_RESPONSE_STATE` — SUPPORTED operationally; prospective biological prediction is improving but no universal validated baseline classifier exists.
5. `DEFICIT_NEGATIVE_PHENOTYPE` — PARTIALLY SUPPORTED as phenotype/dimension; primary-versus-secondary negative symptoms remain a central validity problem.
6. `NEUROIMAGING_SUBTYPES` — CONTESTED/PROVISIONAL; very large cross-site subtyping results coexist with a 2026 field-level review finding no consistently reproduced macroscale subtype profile.
7. `IMMUNE_INFLAMMATORY_ENDOTYPES` — PROVISIONAL; treatment and clozapine exposure remain important temporality/confounding concerns.
8. `DSM_TRADITIONAL_SUBTYPES` — NOT SUPPORTED.

## Saturation

The last material refinement occurred when the recursive search added stronger schizophrenia-internal executive-function subtype evidence and the rare-etiologic layer. Three subsequent targeted cycles produced no change in the class-level interpretation. The saturation rule was therefore met for the primary subtype classification within the web-accessible scope.

This is not a claim of exhaustive PubMed/Embase/PsycINFO coverage.
