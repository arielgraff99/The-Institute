# Branch A Seed — Schizophrenia–Bipolar / Affective Psychosis

Status: OPEN / recursive review not saturated.

## Provisional result

The schizophrenia–bipolar boundary is not supported as a simple pair of wholly independent biological diseases, but neither is a single undifferentiated psychosis disease supported.

Current best representation: **COMPOSITE_MULTIAXIAL / PARTIALLY SUPPORTED SPECTRUM WITH SHARED AND DISORDER-PREDOMINANT COMPONENTS**.

### Evidence favoring shared structure

- Cross-disorder genomics places schizophrenia and bipolar disorder on a common latent genomic factor with extensive common-variant overlap and few disorder-specific loci.
- B-SNIP biomarker-defined psychosis Biotypes cross schizophrenia, schizoaffective disorder and psychotic bipolar disorder rather than reproducing DSM diagnoses.
- Schizoaffective disorder has lower diagnostic reliability than schizophrenia or bipolar disorder and behaves as a boundary/intermediate condition in multiple historical literatures.

### Evidence against complete merger

- Schizophrenia and bipolar disorder retain clinically meaningful differences in cognition, comorbidity, longitudinal course and treatment response.
- Recent genomic decomposition work reports both a shared psychosis component and schizophrenia-predominant / bipolar-predominant genetic components.
- Diagnostic stability is higher for schizophrenia-spectrum and affective-spectrum psychoses than for several boundary categories, suggesting that the current distinction is not purely random noise.

## Frozen-framework interpretation

- DISTINCT_DISEASE binary SCZ vs BD: **NOT YET SUPPORTED AS A CLEAN BIOLOGICAL DICHOTOMY**.
- DIMENSION_OR_SPECTRUM across affective–psychotic liability: **PARTIALLY SUPPORTED**.
- BIOLOGICAL_SUBTYPE_OR_ENDOTYPE: **PROVISIONAL**, especially transdiagnostic psychosis Biotypes and shared/disorder-predominant genetic components.
- OPERATIONAL_CLINICAL_CATEGORY: **SUPPORTED/LIKELY**, because course and treatment differences remain clinically useful.
- COMPOSITE_MULTIAXIAL: **CURRENT LEADING MODEL**.
