# Mental Illness Umbrella Review v0.1 — Seed Classification

Run date: 2026-08-04
Historical ruler: frozen Historical Disease-Splitting Framework v0.2
Status: STARTED / SEED PASS; not a saturated review.

## Root result

Treating all mental illness as one disease is not supported. The best provisional root representation is **COMPOSITE_MULTIAXIAL**.

The first evidence pass does not justify replacing DSM/ICD with a single alternative tree. Instead it identifies several different forms of structure that must be tested independently.

## Provisional architecture

### Layer 1 — etiologic peel-off

Where a positive intrinsic etiology meets the frozen distinct-disease gates, the psychiatric presentation should be reclassified as an etiologically defined disease rather than retained solely as a symptom-defined psychiatric subtype. Current examples from the Institute schizophrenia work include rare genetic and immune causes presenting with psychosis.

### Layer 2 — transdiagnostic liability structure

Common-variant genetics currently supports at least five candidate transdiagnostic liability factors: Compulsive, schizophrenia-bipolar, neurodevelopmental, internalizing, and substance-use. These are candidate biological partitions, not yet disease entities.

### Layer 3 — cross-cutting endotypes

Biological endotypes/biotypes may cut across legacy diagnoses. The schizophrenia and depression modules already provide examples in which the strongest candidate biology does not reproduce the original diagnostic or treatment-resistance boundary.

### Layer 4 — dimensional phenotype

Symptom/cognitive covariance and clinical-neuroscience evidence support dimensional structure such as internalizing, externalizing, thought/psychosis-related, detachment, cognition and other domains. The exact hierarchy is not frozen because current factor systems disagree in some regions and depend on measurement/population.

### Layer 5 — development, trajectory and stage

Age of onset, developmental course, episodicity, persistence, recurrence, deterioration/recovery and other longitudinal structures should be represented independently of etiologic/endotype classification.

### Layer 6 — treatment-response states

TRS and TRD demonstrate that treatment-response status can be clinically important without being a separate disease. Adequate exposure and pseudoresistance controls are mandatory.

### Layer 7 — operational diagnoses and context

Legacy diagnoses remain useful operational categories when they improve communication or care. Social/environmental/health-system context is represented as a modifier and confounder, not intrinsic biological ontology.

## Current top-level candidate map

4. Neurodevelopmental liability — provisional, with important cross-loading and heterogeneity.
5. Compulsive liability — provisional transdiagnostic genetic/endotype factor.
6. Substance-use liability — provisional shared liability plus substance-specific etiologies.
7. Externalizing/disinhibition/antagonism — partially supported dimension/spectrum.
8. General psychopathology liability — provisional broad dimension; not disease ontology.
9. Cross-cutting biological endotypes — provisional and likely central.
10. Symptom/cognitive dimensions — partially supported.
11. Developmental/course stages — provisional orthogonal axis.
12. Treatment-response states — partially supported orthogonal axis.
13. Legacy diagnoses — operational categories pending family-by-family adjudication.
14. Context — modifier/confounder axis only.
