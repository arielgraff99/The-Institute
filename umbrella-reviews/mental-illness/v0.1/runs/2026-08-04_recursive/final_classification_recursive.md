# Mental Illness Umbrella Review v0.1 — Recursive Classification

Run date: 2026-08-04
Historical ruler: frozen Historical Disease-Splitting Framework v0.2
Scope: recursive six-branch web-accessible evidence map with contradiction/exception searches and saturation stopping. This is not a licensed-database systematic review.

## Root result

**Treating mental illness as one disease is not supported.**

Current evidence also does **not** support replacing DSM/ICD with one new mutually exclusive biological tree. The best-supported root representation is a **COMPOSITE_MULTIAXIAL_NETWORK** in which different scientific structures answer different questions.

## Integrated architecture

### Layer 1 — Etiologic disease peel-off

A minority of psychiatric presentations can be reassigned to etiologically defined genetic, immune, neurologic, metabolic/endocrine, infectious, toxic or neurodegenerative diseases when the frozen distinct-disease gates are met. This is the layer most analogous to successful disease splitting elsewhere in medicine.

The key safeguard is positive causality/specificity: a marker, exposure or antibody does not create a new disease unless the discriminator is reproducible, temporally coherent, specific beyond alternatives and supported by intrinsic etiologic/pathological/mechanistic evidence.

### Layer 2 — Transdiagnostic biological liability structure

Large cross-disorder genomic work supports broad shared liabilities, especially schizophrenia-bipolar, internalizing, neurodevelopmental, compulsive and substance-use/externalizing factors. These are useful biological coordinates but are **not five replacement diseases**.

Reasons they are not promoted to diseases include cross-loading, residual disorder-specific genetic components, different developmental/course patterns, incomplete alignment with symptom dimensions, and absent treatment-by-factor clinical utility.

### Layer 3 — Cross-cutting biological endotypes

Biological endotypes/biotypes can be stronger than legacy diagnoses while crossing them. Institute depression and schizophrenia modules already show this pattern. Endotypes should therefore be represented independently from diagnosis and promoted only after replication, temporal validation and ideally treatment-by-endotype utility.

### Layer 4 — Phenotypic dimensions

The evidence supports quantitative dimensions such as psychosis/thought disturbance, internalizing distress/fear, compulsivity, grooming, disinhibition, antagonism, cognition, negative symptoms and mood activation. A patient may occupy several dimensions simultaneously; dimensions should not be forced into mutually exclusive diseases.

### Layer 5 — Development, trajectory and stage

Age of onset, developmental pattern, episodicity, persistence, recurrence, progression/recovery and stage are orthogonal to etiologic/endotype classification. Longitudinal movement is expected and is not evidence that the classification is invalid.

### Layer 6 — Treatment-response states

Treatment resistance and response are separate axes requiring adequate exposure and pseudoresistance control. Prior Institute modules support TRD and TRS as clinically useful treatment-course states without establishing each as one biological disease.

### Layer 7 — Operational diagnoses and context

Legacy diagnoses remain potentially useful for communication, prognosis, treatment pathways and service organization. Their utility does not prove natural disease ontology. Social, environmental and health-system variables are modeled as causes/modifiers/confounders of expression, exposure and observed outcome rather than as intrinsic ontology by default.

## Branch A — Schizophrenia, bipolar disorder and affective psychosis

**Result: SUPPORTED_SHARED_LIABILITY_WITH_DISORDER_PREDOMINANT_COMPONENTS.**

A simple Kraepelinian binary is not supported as two completely isolated biological diseases, but complete merger into one psychosis disease is also not supported. Current evidence fits a shared schizophrenia-bipolar liability plus schizophrenia-predominant and bipolar-predominant components. Schizoaffective disorder functions as a boundary-stress category, while transdiagnostic psychosis biotypes cross conventional diagnoses.

Preferred representation: **DIMENSION_OR_SPECTRUM + BIOLOGICAL_ENDOTYPE + OPERATIONAL_DIAGNOSES**.

## Branch B — Internalizing

**Result: SUPPORTED_INTERNALIZING_SUPERSPECTRUM.**

Depression, generalized anxiety, PTSD and fear-related disorders share substantial common liability. Fear/distress substructure and meaningful disorder-specific components remain. The evidence therefore supports an internalizing spectrum rather than either fully independent diseases or one homogeneous internalizing disease.

Preferred representation: **DIMENSION_OR_SPECTRUM + DISORDER_SPECIFIC_COMPONENTS + OPERATIONAL_DIAGNOSES**.

## Branch C — Neurodevelopmental

**Result: PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED_NEURODEVELOPMENTAL_SUPERSPECTRUM.**

ASD, ADHD and Tourette-related phenotypes share developmental/genetic liability, but differentiating loci, rare variants, disorder-specific pathways, cross-loading and diagnostic-boundary changes prevent promotion of one neurodevelopmental disease. Some patients may instead peel off into positive etiologic diagnoses.

Preferred representation: **OVERLAPPING_DEVELOPMENTAL_LIABILITIES + ETIOLOGIC_SUBGROUPS + DIMENSIONS**.

## Branch D — Compulsive

**Result: SUPPORTED_COMPULSIVITY_AND_GROOMING_DIMENSIONS.**

The last material refinement in the recursive run was evidence that obsessive-compulsive-related disorders are better represented by at least two correlated dimensions: a compulsivity factor and a grooming factor. OCD, anorexia and Tourette-related biology overlap, but important specificity remains; anorexia in particular contains substantial metabolic biology that is not reducible to compulsivity.

Preferred representation: **DIMENSION_OR_SPECTRUM + CROSS_CUTTING_ENDOTYPES**.

## Branch E — Externalizing, substance use and personality pathology

**Result: SUPPORTED_EXTERNALIZING_SUPERSPECTRUM.**

Disinhibition/externalizing liability is strongly supported, but substance-use disorders retain substance-specific and internalizing components, and personality pathology such as borderline features cross-loads onto internalizing, externalizing and other liabilities. The branch therefore behaves as a superspectrum with nested and cross-cutting components, not one disease.

Preferred representation: **DIMENSION_OR_SPECTRUM + SUBSTANCE_SPECIFIC_COMPONENTS + CONTEXT**.

## Branch F — Etiologic peel-off

**Result: SUPPORTED_TRUE_DISEASE_LAYER_AS_SUBSET.**

Some psychiatric presentations are manifestations of identifiable genetic, autoimmune, neurologic, endocrine/metabolic, infectious, toxic, substance-related or neurodegenerative causes. These should be separated when causal criteria are met. Diagnostic yield is highly population- and phenotype-dependent, so targeted workup is supported more strongly than indiscriminate testing.

Preferred representation: **DISTINCT_DISEASE_WHEN_CAUSAL_GATES_MET + TARGETED_DIAGNOSTIC_WORKUP**.

## General psychopathology or p factor

A general psychopathology factor is retained as a broad liability/burden or severity dimension. Statistical reproducibility does not establish that p is one disease or one mechanism. Its biological and causal interpretation remains insufficiently specific for disease ontology.

## What happens to current diagnoses?

This run does not conclude that DSM/ICD diagnoses are useless. Instead, each diagnosis must survive a separate historical-gate test. A current diagnosis may ultimately be:

1. retained as a distinct disease;
2. split because specific etiologies peel away;
3. merged into a spectrum with neighboring diagnoses;
4. retained as an operational clinical syndrome;
5. crossed by stronger biological endotypes;
6. represented partly by quantitative dimensions;
7. supplemented by stage/trajectory and treatment-response axes;
8. or assigned a multiaxial combination of these outcomes.

## Saturation

Six recursive cycles were run across six branches. Cycle 3 produced the last material class refinement, separating the compulsive branch into compulsivity and grooming dimensions. Cycles 4, 5 and 6 added recent evidence, replication checks, failure/contradiction searches and focused falsification but did not change any branch-level class conclusion. The prespecified requirement of three consecutive no-change cycles was therefore met for this web-accessible umbrella run.

## Current mental-illness model

A compact representation is:

**etiologically defined diseases where identifiable**
+
**overlapping transdiagnostic biological liabilities/endotypes**
+
**dimensional symptom/cognitive expression**
+
**developmental and longitudinal trajectory/stage**
+
**treatment-response states**
+
**operational diagnoses and contextual modifiers**
=
**current psychiatric illness space**.

The most important implication is that psychiatry may not primarily reorganize by subdividing every legacy disorder into smaller versions of itself. Some patients may peel off into etiologically defined diseases, some biology may organize transdiagnostically, and much of the remaining variation may require dimensions, trajectories, stages and treatment-response states.

## Next phase

The next Institute step should be a **diagnosis-survival matrix** across major DSM/ICD families. Each diagnosis should be tested for: distinct-disease survival, spectrum/merger evidence, etiologic peel-offs, cross-cutting endotypes, dimensional structure, stage/trajectory, treatment-response states, operational utility, and target-family holdout sensitivity.

## Limitations

This is a recursively saturated evidence map for the branch-level classification within web-accessible sources, not a formal exhaustive systematic review across licensed databases. The selected ledger contains high-information evidence rather than every screened publication. No duplicate human screening or study-family-reconstructed quantitative meta-analysis was performed. Reviews and meta-analyses can overlap primary cohorts. Token, cost and wall-time telemetry were unavailable and were not invented.
