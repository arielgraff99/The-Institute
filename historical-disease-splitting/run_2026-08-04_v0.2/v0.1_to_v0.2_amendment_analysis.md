# v0.1 → v0.2 Amendment Analysis

Run date: 2026-08-04
Base: Historical Disease-Splitting Framework v0.1
Amended output: v0.2

## Purpose

v0.2 was designed as a prospective stress test of v0.1 rather than an accumulation of favorable examples. Nineteen additional historical comparators were selected to challenge the framework with spectra, residual categories, re-merged classifications, low-resource and non-Western settings, technology-driven distinctions, treatment-linked subtypes and country- or regulator-sensitive adoption histories.

The combined evidence map contains 40 historical classification events.

## Amendment decisions

### A1 — Add DIMENSION_OR_SPECTRUM

**Decision:** PROMOTE.

**Triggering evidence:** The Ridley-Jopling leprosy classification demonstrated that an ordered immunological/clinicopathological continuum can be scientifically coherent without natural categorical boundaries. Axial spondyloarthritis and heart-failure EF phenotypes further showed that useful thresholds may sit on underlying continua.

**Reason v0.1 was incomplete:** U03 correctly warned against arbitrary categorical thresholds but offered no positive ontological destination for a validated continuum. A framework that only accepts disease, subtype, stage, resistance or operational category risks converting a dimensional truth into an artificial categorical split.

**v0.2 response:** Add `DIMENSION_OR_SPECTRUM` with a reproducible ordered-gradient requirement and explicit threshold transparency.

### A2 — Add U08 Convergent multi-domain coherence

**Decision:** PROMOTE for biological-ontology claims.

**Triggering evidence:** Strong historical entities such as CML, GIST, APL, NMOSD, MODY, driver-defined NSCLC and low-/high-grade serous ovarian carcinoma became compelling when independent domains converged: morphology/pathology, molecular or causal biology, natural history and/or mechanism-linked treatment response. Failed or revised splits such as low-renin hypertension, SCLC over-subtyping and papillary-RCC type 1/2 were less coherent across domains.

**Qualification:** A single discriminator may occasionally be sufficiently causal and specific to carry unusually high evidentiary weight. U08 therefore requires convergence generally rather than mechanically counting domains.

### A3 — Add U09 Positive-definition / residual-category caution

**Decision:** PROMOTE conditionally.

**Triggering evidence:** Narcolepsy type 1 has strong orexin/hypocretin coherence whereas type 2 lacks an equivalent positive defining biomarker and shows materially lower repeated-test stability. Seronegative RA remains heterogeneous despite a coherent seropositive group. T2-low asthma is less biologically unified than T2-high asthma. The former papillary-RCC type 2 bucket proved molecularly heterogeneous and the binary type 1/type 2 classification was removed.

**Rule:** Coherence of subgroup A does not establish coherence of 'not A'. Marker-negative/NOS/non-A categories require positive reproducible features before promotion to distinct disease or endotype.

### A4 — Add COMPOSITE_MULTIAXIAL output

**Decision:** PROMOTE as a structural rule.

**Triggering evidence:** Many successful classifications are orthogonal rather than mutually exclusive. CML can be a molecularly defined disease, have longitudinal phases and later acquire TKI-resistance states. Cancer can have molecular subtype and anatomical stage simultaneously. Leprosy has an immunological spectrum while acute reaction states can be superimposed.

**Rule:** Downstream adjudication must allow more than one valid axis instead of forcing one winning category.

### A5 — Add H07 Research-opportunity asymmetry

**Decision:** PROMOTE as a context control, not a disease criterion.

**Triggering evidence:** Type 5/malnutrition-related diabetes has been described for decades in lean, undernourished populations, was classified by WHO in 1985, removed in 1999 amid causal uncertainty, and re-recognized in 2025 as modern metabolic studies accumulated. Burkitt lymphoma was first recognized in East Africa and later transformed by molecular/viral evidence. HIV-2 and other geographically concentrated diseases likewise illustrate detection dependence on surveillance and research networks.

**Rule:** Sparse or delayed evidence from underfunded populations weakens negative historical inference. It does not create positive evidence of a distinct disease.

### A6 — Add H08 Jurisdictional adoption divergence

**Decision:** PROMOTE as a context control.

**Triggering evidence:** Historical classifications can be adopted, removed or retained differently by WHO, national regulators, professional societies or healthcare systems. Such divergence may reflect service organization, reimbursement, local epidemiology, regulatory standards or available technology.

**Rule:** Scientific validity and jurisdiction-specific adoption are recorded on separate axes.

### A7 — Add target-family holdout sensitivity

**Decision:** PROMOTE as anti-circularity safeguard.

**Reason:** A downstream review of schizophrenia, diabetes or another disorder family could otherwise be judged partly by rules derived from that same family, creating subtle criterion leakage.

**Rule:** When close historical comparators exist, repeat adjudication excluding those comparator families and report whether the conclusion changes.

## v0.1 criteria that survived unchanged in substance

The expanded history did not overturn:

- U01 operationalizable/reproducible discriminator;
- U02 independent replication/transportability;
- U03 boundary robustness;
- U04 temporal coherence appropriate to classification type;
- U05 specificity beyond alternatives;
- U06 natural-history/longitudinal coherence appropriate to class;
- U07 clinical decision utility, supportive rather than ontological proof;
- the requirement that treatment-resistance states follow adequate treatment exposure;
- explicit assessment of pseudoresistance/adherence/exposure/system causes;
- the distinction between useful operational categories and biological disease entities;
- the rule that regulation, consensus, historical persistence, technology or treatment response alone cannot establish disease ontology.

## What the added negative/revised controls taught

1. **A biologically coherent positive subgroup can coexist with a heterogeneous residual group.** Do not infer two diseases from one validated marker-positive group.
2. **Modern molecular technology can invalidate older morphology or geography-based categories rather than merely refine them.**
3. **Consensus can simplify over-classification as well as create classification.** Sepsis and papillary-RCC revisions show that re-merging can be evidence-based.
4. **Continuous biology is not failed classification.** Sometimes the correct result is a spectrum.
5. **A clinically useful threshold may remain operational even when biology is continuous.**
6. **Context can create evidentiary asymmetry.** Lack of evidence is less informative when the population has had little access to research infrastructure.

## Magnitude of amendment

**Framework impact:** MODERATE.

v0.2 does not replace the core v0.1 ruler. It makes it harder to over-call categorical disease splits and gives the framework positive destinations for spectra and multi-axial classifications.

The additions most likely to affect downstream psychiatric reviews are:

- explicit testing of dimensions/spectra;
- residual-category caution;
- multi-domain convergence before strong biological ontology claims;
- multi-axial outcomes;
- target-family holdout sensitivity.

## Required downstream comparison

Any disease-specific analysis previously performed under v0.1 that could plausibly be affected by these additions should report a v0.1 → v0.2 delta. The earlier conclusion should not be silently overwritten.
